Privacy Policy — ICT 1110

University of Zambia · First-Year ICT Study App

Effective Date: April 23, 2025  ·  Last Updated: April 23, 2025

📋 Overview

ICT 1110 is an educational Android application developed by George Best Jr III (GeorgeBest) to help first-year ICT students at the University of Zambia access quizzes, past tests, exam papers, and recommended books.

This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected when you use the app, how it is used, and your rights. By using ICT 1110 you agree to the practices described here.

🔍 Information We Collect

ICT 1110 does not require you to create an account or provide personal information to use the app. No name, email address, or phone number is collected by us directly.

The following data may be collected automatically through third-party services:

  • Device information (model, OS version, language) — collected by Google AdMob for ad delivery
  • Advertising ID (Google AAID) — used to show relevant, non-personalised ads
  • Approximate location (country/region) — inferred by AdMob from IP address for ad targeting
  • App interaction data — aggregated usage statistics via AdMob SDK

⚙️ How We Use Information

Any data collected is used solely to:

  • Display advertisements through Google AdMob to keep the app free
  • Improve ad relevance and measure ad performance
  • Comply with legal obligations

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with any third party outside the services described below.

🔗 Third-Party Services

ICT 1110 integrates the following third-party SDKs:

  • Google AdMob — serves in-app advertisements and may collect device & ad-interaction data
  • URL Launcher — opens links in your device's browser; no data is collected by us from this
  • Share Plus — allows you to share content via your device's share sheet; no data is sent to our servers
Google AdMob is governed by Google's Privacy Policy: policies.google.com/privacy . We have no control over the data Google collects through AdMob.

🎓 Children's Privacy

ICT 1110 is designed for university students (18 years and older). The app is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child under 13 has used this app and provided personal data, please contact us immediately so we can take appropriate action.

🔒 Data Storage & Security

ICT 1110 does not operate its own backend server. All app content (study materials, quizzes, past papers) is bundled within the app itself and stored locally on your device.

No user data is transmitted to or stored on servers owned by the developer. Data handled by third-party SDKs (e.g., AdMob) is subject to their own security and retention policies.

📱 App Permissions

ICT 1110 may request the following Android permissions:

  • INTERNET — required to load advertisements via AdMob
  • ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE — checks connectivity before loading ads

No other sensitive permissions (camera, microphone, contacts, location) are requested.

✅ Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Opt out of personalised ads by resetting your Google Advertising ID in your device settings
  • Request information about data collected about you by contacting us
  • Uninstall the app at any time to stop all data collection

To manage Google's ad personalisation settings, visit adssettings.google.com.

🔄 Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with a new effective date. Continued use of the app after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
